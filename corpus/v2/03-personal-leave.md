# Personal Leave Policy

## Purpose

Personal leave supports an employee who cannot work because of their own illness or injury, or who
must provide care or support to an immediate family or household member.

## Notification

Employees should notify their team as soon as reasonably possible and provide the expected period
of absence. The synthetic company does not require medical details in a team chat.

## Evidence

The fictional People Operations team may request reasonable evidence for an extended or repeated
absence. Evidence should be supplied through the designated private channel rather than pasted
into the assistant.
