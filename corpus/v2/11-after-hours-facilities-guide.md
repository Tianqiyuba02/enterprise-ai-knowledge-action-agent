# Melbourne Facilities After-Hours Guide

## Escort requirement

Employees entering the fictional Melbourne office before 8:00 am or after 6:00 pm must arrange an
approved facilities escort, even when their building pass is active.

## Requests

Escort requests should be sent to the fictional Facilities desk before 3:00 pm on the preceding
business day.
