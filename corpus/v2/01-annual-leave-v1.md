# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Eligible full-time employees receive eighteen days of paid annual leave for each completed year of
service. Part-time employees receive the equivalent entitlement on a proportional basis.

## Requests

Employees should submit planned leave at least ten calendar days before the first day away. A
request is not approved until the employee receives confirmation through the fictional leave
system.

## Balance

The current balance shown in the fictional employee portal is the operational balance. Employees
should contact the synthetic People Operations team if a balance appears incorrect.
