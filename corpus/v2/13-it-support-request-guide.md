# IT Support Request and Triage Guide

## Reporting an issue

Employees should describe what they were trying to do, what happened, and any safe error message
shown. Do not include passwords, recovery codes, API keys, or other secret values. Creating a draft
does not send a request; the employee must review and explicitly submit it in the portal.

## Category guidance

Use **access** for sign-in or permission issues, **hardware** for physical devices, **software** for
applications, and **network** for connectivity. Choose the category that best matches the main
symptom; the fictional IT team may correct it during triage.

## Urgency guidance

Use **high** when essential work is blocked and there is no safe workaround, **medium** when work
is materially disrupted but can continue, and **low** for minor issues or planned help. Urgency
describes impact; it does not guarantee a response time or override IT triage.

## Support process

After submission, the portal shows the employee-visible ticket number and status. Employees can
use My Tickets to review their own requests. A ticket must never be used to send secrets or private
credentials.
