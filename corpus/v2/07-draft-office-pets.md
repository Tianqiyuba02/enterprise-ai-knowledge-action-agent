# Draft Office Animals Proposal

## Unapproved proposal

This draft explores whether employees could occasionally bring companion animals to a designated
Melbourne office area. It has not been approved and must not be used as company policy.

## Open questions

Facilities has not resolved allergies, insurance, cleaning, or building-access requirements. Until
an approved policy exists, the assistant should not claim that office animals are permitted.
