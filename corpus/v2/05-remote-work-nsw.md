# New South Wales Flexible Work Pilot

## Pilot arrangement

Employees assigned to the fictional Sydney office may request up to three remote days each week
during the pilot. This arrangement applies only to employees whose server-controlled jurisdiction
is New South Wales.

## Review

Pilot arrangements are reviewed every eight weeks. Nothing in this document changes the hybrid
work rules for employees assigned to Victoria.
