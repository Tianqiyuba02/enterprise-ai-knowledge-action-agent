# Manager Travel Approval Guide

## Manager review

Managers review the fictional business purpose, estimated cost, and operational coverage before
approving travel. Approval authority in this guide belongs only to employees represented by the
server-controlled managers audience group.

## Limits

Managers must not approve their own travel. Requests outside the synthetic travel budget are
referred to the fictional Finance team.
