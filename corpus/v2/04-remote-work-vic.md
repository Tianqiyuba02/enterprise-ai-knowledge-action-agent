# Victorian Hybrid Work Policy

## Standard arrangement

Melbourne-based employees may work remotely for up to two regular days each week when their role
and team commitments permit. Teams publish shared collaboration days so that customer, training,
and planning work can be completed in person.

## Changes to location

A regular remote-work location must remain within Victoria unless a separate temporary-work
arrangement is approved. Employees should tell their manager before changing the usual location
recorded in the fictional employee portal.

## Equipment and safety

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe workspace and protecting company information.
The synthetic IT team supplies standard laptop equipment but does not reimburse unapproved home
renovations or furniture purchases.
