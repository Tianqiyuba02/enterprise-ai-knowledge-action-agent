# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Eligible full-time employees receive twenty days of paid annual leave for each completed year of
service. Part-time employees receive the equivalent entitlement in proportion to their ordinary
working pattern.

## Planning leave

Employees should discuss foreseeable absences with their team before submitting a request.
Requests for five or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least fourteen
calendar days before the first day away. Shorter requests should be submitted as early as
practicable.

## Approval and balances

Submitting a request does not itself approve leave. Approval is recorded only in the fictional
leave system. The current portal balance is measured in hours and is the balance used when checking
whether a request can be supported.

## Carry-over

Unused leave may carry into the next calendar year. A manager may ask an employee with a high
balance to prepare a reasonable leave plan, but the manager cannot remove an accrued balance
through an informal conversation.
