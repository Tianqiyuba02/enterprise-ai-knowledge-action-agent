# After-Hours Office Access Policy

## Access window

Melbourne employees with an active building pass may enter the fictional office between 6:00 am
and 10:00 pm on any day without arranging a separate escort.

## Safety

Employees entering outside normal team hours should sign in at reception and follow the lone-worker
check-in procedure.
